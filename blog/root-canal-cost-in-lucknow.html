Root Canal Cost in Lucknow: A Complete Price Guide

PP
Dr. Priyank Prakash
• Updated Sept 2026 • 6 min read

"How much will a root canal cost me?" is one of the first questions patients ask when a dentist recommends RCT (root canal treatment). It's a fair question — quotes across Lucknow can vary quite a bit, and it's not always obvious why. Here's a clear breakdown of what actually drives root canal cost, so you know exactly what you're paying for before you sit in the chair.

What Is a Root Canal, Briefly

A root canal treats a tooth where the inner pulp — the soft tissue containing nerves and blood vessels — has become infected or inflamed, usually from deep decay, a crack, or repeated dental procedures on the same tooth. The infected tissue is removed, the canal is cleaned and shaped, and then sealed to prevent further infection — saving the natural tooth instead of extracting it.

What Actually Affects Root Canal Cost

1. Which Tooth Is Being Treated

Front teeth (incisors and canines) usually have a single canal and are quicker and less expensive to treat. Molars at the back can have 3–4 canals, more complex anatomy, and take longer — which naturally costs more.

2. First-Time Treatment vs. Re-Treatment

A root canal re-treatment (retreating a tooth that had RCT done before, elsewhere, that has failed or reinfected) is more technically demanding and typically costs more than a first-time procedure.

3. Technique & Equipment

Clinics using rotary endodontic systems and digital imaging (like RVG X-rays) can work more precisely and often in fewer sittings, which affects pricing versus older manual techniques.

4. Number of Sittings

Simple cases can sometimes be completed in a single sitting; infected or complex cases may need 2–3 visits with medication placed in between to fully clear infection before final sealing.

5. The Crown (Cap) Afterward

This is the part patients most often miss. After a root canal, the tooth is usually weaker and needs a crown to protect it from fracturing under chewing pressure. The root canal procedure and the crown are typically priced separately — so a quote that only covers the RCT itself isn't your full cost.

Dr. Priyank Prakash's tip: When comparing quotes between clinics, always ask two questions: does this include the final crown, and what technique is being used? A cheaper quote that excludes the crown or uses outdated equipment often ends up costing more in the long run.

Typical Cost Ranges

Exact pricing depends on your specific tooth and case, but here's a general sense of how root canal costs are usually structured in Lucknow:

ComponentWhat It Covers
Front tooth RCTSingle canal, generally the most affordable option
Premolar RCT1–2 canals, moderate complexity
Molar RCT3–4 canals, most time-intensive, priced higher
Re-treatment (RCT redo)Higher than first-time treatment due to added complexity
Crown/capPriced separately based on material (ceramic, metal-ceramic, zirconia, etc.)

For an exact, itemized quote for your specific tooth, the most reliable step is a consultation and X-ray — pricing without seeing the tooth is always just a rough estimate.

Why "Cheapest" Isn't Always Best Value

A very low quote can sometimes mean the crown isn't included, older manual instruments are being used, or fewer canals are being properly cleaned than the tooth actually has — which raises the risk of the treatment failing and needing a retreatment (which costs more overall than doing it right the first time).

How to Get an Accurate Quote

  1. Book a consultation with an X-ray so the dentist can see the exact tooth and canal structure
  2. Ask whether the quote includes the final crown
  3. Ask how many sittings are expected
  4. Ask what equipment/technique will be used

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost of a root canal in Lucknow?

Root canal cost varies depending on the tooth (front tooth vs. molar), number of canals, whether it's a re-treatment, and the crown you choose afterward. Book a consultation and X-ray at Brite Smile Dental Care for an exact quote.

Why does root canal cost vary so much between clinics?

Cost differences usually come down to the equipment used (rotary vs. manual), the dentist's experience, the number of sittings, and the type of crown placed after treatment. Very low quotes often exclude the final crown or use older techniques.

Does root canal cost include the crown (cap)?

Not always. The root canal procedure and the crown that protects the tooth afterward are usually priced separately. Always ask your dentist whether the quote includes the crown, as skipping it can put the tooth at risk of fracture.

Is root canal treatment covered by dental insurance in India?

Many health and dental insurance plans in India partially cover root canal treatment, though coverage varies by insurer and policy. Check your policy documents or ask our front desk for help with claims.
