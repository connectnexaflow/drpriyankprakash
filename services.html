Teeth Whitening

Safe, fast, in-clinic whitening that removes deep stains from tea, coffee, and tobacco — a noticeably brighter smile in a single visit.

Dental Implants

Titanium implant technology that restores your smile, bite strength, and confidence — the most permanent solution for a missing tooth.

Check-ups & Digital X-Rays

Thorough exams, digital X-rays, and gum health assessments — recommended every 6 months to catch problems early.

Orthodontics & Invisalign

Metal, ceramic braces, and Invisalign clear aligners for teens and adults — straighter teeth without disrupting daily life.

Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Pain-free RCT using rotary endodontics and advanced anaesthesia — saving your natural tooth in as few visits as possible.

Scaling, root planing, and periodontal therapy for bleeding gums, bad breath, or looseness before they progress further.

Wisdom Tooth Removal

Crowns, Bridges & Dentures

Paediatric Dentistry
